What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is made up of computer systems designed to do tasks that normally need human intelligence.

📚

Learning

Improving from data and experience instead of following fixed rules only.

🧠

Reasoning

Working through information to reach sensible conclusions.

🧩

Problem-solving

Finding ways to get from a question or a challenge to an answer.

⚖️

Decision-making

Choosing an action, such as which song or product to suggest next.

What makes AI so subtle is that most people use it every day without even noticing.

AI in daily life

Here are some places you probably meet AI already.

🎬

Streaming

Netflix and Apple Music use AI to recommend movies and music based on what you like.

🗣️

Virtual assistants

Siri, Alexa and ChatGPT help with reminders, searches and controlling smart devices.

🛒

Online shopping

AI suggests products, manages inventory and powers customer-service assistants, which do not always work perfectly.

🚗

Transport

Navigation apps and self-driving cars aim to make travel safer and more efficient.

AI in education and healthcare

🎓

Education

AI-powered apps can give learners personalised tutoring and support, so students can get help at their own pace.

🩺

Healthcare

AI may help doctors with medical diagnoses. Many people are still deciding how much they trust it for something so important.

Benefits and risks

AI makes life more convenient, efficient and personalised, but it also raises serious concerns.

✅ Benefits

  • Saves time and makes everyday tasks easier
  • Personalised recommendations and learning
  • Safer, more efficient travel
  • Support for people in education and healthcare

⚠️ Risks

  • Jobs: automation may replace some human work and affect the economy
  • Privacy: AI analyses your data, and most people agree to this in the terms and conditions without a real choice
  • Bias: algorithms can sometimes reinforce unfairness
  • Ethics: who is responsible when AI gets it wrong?

AI offers many opportunities, but it must be used responsibly so that its benefits outweigh the risks.
